> local intel: what any website can see about you

Only your own device is inspected. The network trace is a single request you trigger, sent to ipwho.is, to look up your own public IP. IP location is approximate (usually city level) and changes with a VPN. The GPS fix needs your explicit permission.

> subject registry

Enter a name and run the registration. The page takes 5 automatic captures from slightly different angles and saves a profile (face signature, thumbnail, estimated age and gender) in this browser only. Register only people who have agreed to it.

Educational demo. Face analysis runs locally in your browser; camera frames are never uploaded. Age and gender are model estimates and can be wrong.
